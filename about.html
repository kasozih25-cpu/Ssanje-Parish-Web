Our Story

About Ssanje Parish

History

The parish was formally established in 1987 during a period of steady pastoral expansion within the diocese under Bishop Adrian Kivumbi Ddungu. Prior to becoming an independent parish, the community was served as a sub-station, receiving sacraments from older neighboring parishes in the Kyotera and Rakai zones.

Over time, the parish grew significantly in structural importance, eventually becoming the headquarters of the Ssanje Deanery. Today, this deanery manages an expansive territory comprising 6 parishes and 77 sub-parishes.

Parish Patron Saint

The parish is dedicated to St. John the Baptist. As the patron saint, his life serves as the theological foundation for the parish's focus on evangelization, baptismal renewal, and preparing the community to live according to the Gospel.

Mission & Vision

Mission

Holistic evangelization, basic Christian community growth, healthcare excellence, and environmental stewardship.

Vision

To be an evangelized and evangelizing Church, United, Co-ordinate, and Self-Sustaining, Rooted in the Basic Christian Community.
