Audible Voices

Giving voice to the voiceless, restoring hope to the forgotten.

Audible Voices is a non-profit organisation dedicated to transforming lives through mental health advocacy, juvenile rehabilitation, and street children outreach. We believe every person deserves a second chance, and we work tirelessly to help individuals break free from addiction, reclaim their dignity, and rebuild their futures.

Our programmes focus on counselling, drug and substance abuse rehabilitation, mentorship for at-risk youth, and reintegration support for street children. We provide a safe space where healing begins and new opportunities take root.

Through Audible Voices, individuals who have completed rehabilitation programmes are connected with GFDI — our sister organisation — where they gain practical agricultural skills, livelihood training, and a pathway to sustainable independence.

Vision

A society where every individual, regardless of their past, has access to healing, opportunity, and a dignified life.

Mission

To restore lives affected by mental health challenges, substance abuse, and street life through compassionate care, rehabilitation, and sustainable reintegration into the community.

How Audible Voices and GFDI Work Together

Audible Voices and GFDI operate as complementary organisations under one shared vision. While Audible Voices focuses on healing and rehabilitation, GFDI provides the next step — equipping rehabilitated individuals with agricultural skills, farm-based livelihoods, and economic independence. Together, they create a complete journey from recovery to self-sufficiency.
