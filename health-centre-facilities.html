Healthcare Services

St. Jude Ssanje Health Centre III

Providing quality healthcare to the Ssanje community and surrounding areas since 2003.

Overview Services People Facilities

Population Served

The health centre serves a population of over 20,000 people from the following sub-counties:

  • Kasasa sub-county
  • Kyebe sub-county
  • Kifamba sub-county
  • Kakuuto sub-county

Supporting Facilities

The health centre is supported by the following sanitation and waste management infrastructure:

  • Clean pit latrine
  • Placenta pit
  • Rubbish pit
  • Incinerator

Utilities

The facility has a reliable supply of water and electricity to all buildings. A backup solar power system is also in place to ensure continuous operations during power outages.

Emergency Contacts

For medical emergencies, contact the health centre directly.

Medical Team

Our team includes medical officers, nurses, laboratory technicians, and pharmacy staff dedicated to quality care.
