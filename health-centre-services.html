Healthcare Services

St. Jude Ssanje Health Centre III

Providing quality healthcare to the Ssanje community and surrounding areas since 2003.

Overview Services People Facilities

Vision, Mission and Core Values

Vision

A community where all people achieve their full potential for health and well-being across the lifespan. The centre aims to be a trusted health centre, a valued community partner, and a creator of positive change.

Mission

To provide client-centered healthcare with excellence in service, quality, and access.

Core Values

God First
Respect for Human Life
Client-Centeredness
Non-Discrimination
Transparency and Accountability
Teamwork

Services Offered

Outpatient Services

General medical consultations and treatment for common illnesses.

Inpatient Services

Bed admission for patients requiring extended medical care.

Laboratory Services

Diagnostic tests including blood work, urine analysis, and malaria testing.

HIV Counselling and Testing

Confidential HIV testing, counseling, and treatment support.

Natural Family Planning

Reproductive health education and natural family planning services.

Immunization

Vaccination programs for children and adults.

Antenatal Care (ANC)

Prenatal check-ups and support for expectant mothers.

Maternity Services

Safe delivery services and postnatal care for mothers and newborns.
