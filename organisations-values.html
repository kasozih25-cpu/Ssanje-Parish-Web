Overall Goal

To contribute to sustainable social, economic and environmental development by investing in people, productive livelihoods, agriculture, communities and natural resources for the benefit of present and future generations.

Objectives

  • Promote sustainable and climate-smart agriculture and resilient food systems.
  • Improve household food and nutrition security.
  • Promote coffee and other agricultural value chains, including quality improvement, traceability, value addition and market access.
  • Support farmer training, demonstration farms, farm recordkeeping and practical agricultural innovation.
  • Promote agroforestry, tree planting, soil and water conservation and environmental restoration.
  • Equip young people with agricultural, vocational, entrepreneurial, digital and leadership skills.
  • Promote women's economic empowerment, enterprise development and participation in leadership.
  • Support community enterprises, savings initiatives, cooperatives and responsible access to markets.
  • Promote education, community learning, research, knowledge exchange and innovation.
  • Promote health, sanitation, hygiene, nutrition and community wellbeing.
  • Support vulnerable households and inclusive participation of persons with disabilities and other marginalised groups.
  • Mobilise lawful resources and partnerships for community development.
  • Work constructively with government, private sector, civil society, academic institutions, farmer organisations and development partners.
  • Promote livelihoods and productive assets that benefit present and future generations.
