Ssanje Farm School

Practical agricultural skills for integral human development, rooted in self-reliance.

About the Farm School

Ssanje Agricultural Farm Institute is located along the Masaka-Mutukula highway in Ssanje trading center, 17 km from Kyotera town. The institute was started by Masaka Diocese and later mandated by the Ministry of Education and Sports in 2019.

The institute is rooted in the spirit of self-reliance — the ability to rely on and utilize our own resources, capabilities, talents, and efforts to grow and develop. The goal is achieved through offering persons practical occupational skills, competence-based assessment, and certification.

Mission

To offer agribusiness training and skilling for employability, agro-entrepreneurship, and integral development.

Vision

To be a leading institution in the provision of innovative practical agricultural skills inspired by values for integral human development.

Admission

Who Can Attend

Open to all persons seeking practical agricultural skills, including school leavers, youth, and community members.

Admission Requirements

  • Completed application form
  • Academic records (for formal students)
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Payment of fees

How to Apply
