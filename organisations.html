Our Organisations

Our work in Ssanje is carried out through two complementary organisations that share a common vision of transforming lives and building sustainable communities.

Audible Voices

Giving voice to the voiceless, restoring hope to the forgotten.

A non-profit organisation dedicated to transforming lives through mental health advocacy, juvenile rehabilitation, and street children outreach. Audible Voices provides counselling, drug and substance abuse rehabilitation, mentorship for at-risk youth, and reintegration support.

Read more →

Generational Farms Development Initiative (GFDI)

A programme under Audible Voices

A community-based development organisation building productive livelihoods, knowledge, healthy communities and resilient natural resources. GFDI serves as a rehabilitation pathway, offering practical agricultural skills and a fresh start through farming.

Read more →

How They Work Together

Audible Voices focuses on healing and rehabilitation, while GFDI provides the next step — equipping rehabilitated individuals with agricultural skills, farm-based livelihoods, and economic independence. Together, they create a complete journey from recovery to self-sufficiency.
